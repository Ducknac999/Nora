========================================
  NORA — your offline AI assistant
========================================

SETUP (only takes a few minutes, one time):

1. RIGHT-CLICK  "Install Nora"  ->  click  "Open".
   (macOS shows a warning the first time — click "Open" to continue.
    You only ever do this once.)

2. Let it finish. It downloads everything for your Mac automatically.
   You need internet for THIS step only. After that, Nora is fully offline.

3. When it says "NORA IS INSTALLED":
   - Drag the  "Nora"  icon into your Dock.
   - Drag the  "Stop Nora"  icon into your Dock too.

DAILY USE:

   - Click  "Nora"       ->  turns the AI ON  (a window opens).
   - Click  "Stop Nora"  ->  turns it OFF  (or just close the window).

That's it. Nora runs 100% on your Mac, no internet needed.

----------------------------------------
Want image understanding too? (optional)
Right-click "Install Vision Update" -> Open, let it finish.
Next time you open Nora, you'll see a "Vision" tab.
